
# Public holidays of Türkiye

Statutory public holidays under Law 2429 (Ulusal Bayram ve Genel Tatiller
Hakkında Kanun), as amended, plus concrete dates for **2026**. Religious feast
dates come from Diyanet's 2026 religious days list; fixed holidays use the
month/day in the law.

| [`datapackage.json`](datapackage.json) | – | Metadata and field schema |

## Notes

- **Religious feasts** start at 13:00 on the eve (`arefe`). For 2026, Diyanet
  lists Ramazan Bayramı 20–22 March and Kurban Bayramı 27–30 May; the
  corresponding arefe days are 19 March and 26 May, matching Law 2429's 3.5 /
  4.5 day durations.
- **Republic Day** begins at 13:00 on 28 October and continues on 29 October.
- Weekends and half-day Saturday rules in Art. 3 are not expanded into rows.
- Kandil nights are not statutory public holidays under Law 2429 and are not
  listed.
